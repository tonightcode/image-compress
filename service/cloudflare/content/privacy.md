# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

This policy explains how letsmall handles information when you use our Windows
image compression app, website, purchase flow and license activation service.
For privacy questions, contact **jialeipengzx@gmail.com**.

## Your images stay on your computer

letsmall compresses images locally. The app does not upload your images, image
contents or image file paths to our servers. You do not need to create an
account to compress images. The app stores settings, free-use counters and,
after activation, a license file locally on your computer.

## Website and service requests

When you visit the website or use an online service, our hosting provider,
Cloudflare, processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the
service, such as IP addresses and request information. Our application uses
hashed IP-based identifiers, request counts and time windows to limit abuse
on purchase, purchase-status and activation endpoints. A hash is not a
guarantee of anonymity.

We do not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies to this website.
Hosting infrastructure may process operational and security information.

## Purchases and activation

Payments are handled through Paddle. Paddle processes the information needed
for checkout, receipts, tax, fraud prevention and refunds under its own terms
and privacy policy. We do not store full payment card numbers or card security
codes in our application database.

Our service stores purchase-session identifiers, hashes of session secrets
and activation tokens, transaction references, product and payment status,
notification identifiers, refund blocks and relevant timestamps. On activation,
the app sends a hashed computer and Windows-user identifier, which the service
stores with the order to enforce the one-computer license. The service does
not receive the underlying Windows machine identifier through that field.

Successful activation saves a signed license locally. Subsequent license
verification and image compression work offline without a routine online
license check.

## Essential cookies

Starting checkout creates an essential purchase cookie to connect payment and
activation to the same browser. It has a maximum lifetime of 24 hours, is
HttpOnly, Secure on the deployed HTTPS site and SameSite=Lax. The activation
link expires separately, 10 minutes after payment confirmation. Clearing or
blocking the cookie may prevent the purchase browser from retrieving the link.
Paddle may use its own cookies or similar technologies within checkout; see
its privacy policy for details.

## Why information is used

We use this information to deliver purchases, issue and verify licenses,
respond to support requests, prevent fraud and misuse, process refunds and
meet applicable obligations. Where a legal basis is required, these purposes
may rely on performing a contract, legitimate interests in securing and
operating the service, or legal obligations, as applicable.

We do not sell your personal information or use purchase or activation data
for targeted advertising. If you email support, we receive your email address
and the information you choose to send. Do not send payment card details,
passwords or private images.

## Service providers and international processing

Cloudflare hosts the website and license service and stores the application
database. Paddle handles checkout and related payment services. Support email
is handled through Gmail. Information may be processed outside your country;
the relevant providers apply their contractual and legal safeguards where
required. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary
to protect the service and users.

Provider information:

- Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- Paddle: https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy

## Retention and security

We retain purchase and license records as needed to operate the license system,
preserve one-time activation history, resolve disputes and meet applicable
obligations. Expiration of a cookie or activation link does not automatically
delete its database record. Support messages and technical records are retained
as needed for their purposes; retention may also depend on provider settings.

We use HTTPS for deployed purchase and activation services, hashed session
secrets, signed licenses and restricted access to service credentials. No
storage or transmission method is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction,
deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, object to certain
processing, or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact
**jialeipengzx@gmail.com** to make a request. We may need to verify your identity;
some records may be retained when required by law or necessary for legitimate
purposes. Contact Paddle for requests concerning information it controls.

Deleting local license data can remove your paid access. A privacy request
does not automatically create a replacement license or waive applicable rights.
